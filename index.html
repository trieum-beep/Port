    🎲 Games on the go

    👀 I spy… in Portuguese
    • "I spy something azul" (blue). Then amarelo (yellow), vermelho (red), verde (green), branco (white).
    • The 5-year-old says the colour, the 3-year-old points.
    🔔 Tram driver
    • One child drives, the others are passengers.
    • "Ding ding!" = everybody freezes. "Próxima paragem!" = go!
    🪜 Stair counter
    • Count steps out loud: um, dois, três…
    • Guess first. Closest guess wins.
    🐟 Fish memory
    • "In the Oceanário I saw a shark…" Each one adds an animal.
    ⚓ Ship explorer
    • The bench is a caravel. Who is captain? "Terra à vista!" (land ahoy) when you see the river.
